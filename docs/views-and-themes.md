# Rotation, views and themes in the Studio

The wall plays one list, the Rotation. Each row of it is a panel in a view, for some seconds. A view is Robotics, Prime or Nova. A theme is a style, and every view looks the same in every theme. The sidebar is in docs/studio-map.md.

## Rotation

Open Rotation, the second line of the sidebar. The wall plays this list top to bottom. Drag a row to move it, or use the Up and Down buttons on a phone. Turn Shown off to skip a row. Seconds is how long it stays. An empty Seconds follows Seconds per page on the Timing tab of Dashboard Settings.

- Copy puts the same row under it. Remove takes a row out. Add a row at the bottom puts a new row at the end.
- More opens the details of a row: the panel, the department of a Monday row, the page of a list (page 1 only, or page 2 on), the small panel beside it, and the photos of a Photo row.
- A change is saved as a draft of Dashboard Settings at once. Click Publish all to put it on the screen.
- The full form of the same list is the field Rotation (full form) of Dashboard Settings. It is hidden there. Use the Rotation page.

## Views

Open Teams and views, then Views, then Robotics, Prime or Nova. Above the map is a picker that moves to another view. The letters of the map open the tabs:

- A Banner: the team, the arrangement (standard or bar), mirror and colors.
- B Main panel: a note that points to Rotation.
- C Countdown: where the countdown sits.
- D Side panel: the small panels. Only the standard arrangement has them.
- E Ticker: a link to Ticker.

Family is not in the rotation. Its page is linked from Kept data, the last line of Coaches only, and it is kept so nothing is lost. A Monday tasks row with no department plays one page for each Team lead that has On the Monday board switched on.

The first time a view is opened with no rows, the Studio fills it from the Screens: rows of Prime go to Prime, rows of Nova to Nova and rows with no team to Robotics. A view that finds no rows starts with the starting rows in studio/tools/view-defaults.js. Click Publish all afterwards.

## Logo and motion

The Look page has the logo, the name effect and the page changes. The banner title for Robotics is in the Banner section of the Timing tab of Dashboard Settings. The time zone is in the dated rules, which Look links to.

## Themes and backgrounds

Open Look. Its first section lists the themes and holds the theme of each day of the week. The second section holds the background of the Original theme: Sweep, Blueprint, Rays, Spotlight or Embers. A change is saved as a draft of Dashboard Settings. Click Publish all. Foundry is not in the starting week; pick it for a day to use it.

## Dated rules

Look links to the dated rules: the color themes (Hawktimus, Neon Prime), the seasonal packs and the time zone. They are in a form of their own. It is not the theme of each day.

## Test the token

Dashboard Settings, Monday tab, Test the token asks the Teletraan computer through the control page who the Monday token belongs to. It shows the account name or the error.

## Not used any more

The rows of a Screen, the weekday schedule of Screens, Panel order, the name of the Robotics view and the name on the screen of a view are hidden and kept, so that nothing already typed is lost.
