# Views and themes in the Studio

A View is what plays on the TV for a few minutes: General, Prime, Nova or Family. A theme is a style, and every view looks the same in every theme.

## Views

Open Settings, then Views, then one of the four pages. Above the map is a picker that moves to another view. The letters of the map open the tabs:

- A Banner: the name in the view tag, the team, the arrangement (standard or bar), mirror, view tag, team chip and colors.
- B Main panel: the minutes the view stays and its rows, top to bottom.
- C Countdown: where the countdown sits.
- D Side panel: the small panels. Only the standard arrangement has them.
- E Ticker: a link to Tips and News.

Family plays Overall progress and Status by department, and its rows are locked. A Monday tasks row with no department plays one page for each Team lead that has On the Monday board switched on.

The first time a view is opened with no rows, the Studio fills it from the Screens: rows of Prime go to Prime, rows of Nova to Nova and rows with no team to General. A view that finds no rows starts with the starting rows in studio/tools/view-defaults.js. Click Publish all afterwards.

## Look tab

Dashboard Settings, Look tab: the name and title of the General view and the order of the views. The time zone stays on the Look page.

## Themes

Settings, then Themes, lists the themes and holds the theme of each day of the week. A change is saved as a draft of Dashboard Settings. Click Publish all. Foundry is not in the starting week; pick it for a day to use it.

## Test the token

Dashboard Settings, Monday tab, Test the token asks the Teletraan computer through the control page who the Monday token belongs to. It shows the account name or the error.

## Not used any more

The rows of a Screen, the weekday schedule of Screens and Panel order are hidden and kept, so that nothing already typed is lost.
