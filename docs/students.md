# Students: changing what is on the TV

For the students and mentors who change what is on the wall TV. You do not need to
program. The TV is called Teletraan I. The Studio is the web page where we type
what it shows. This page has the same parts as the Start here page, the first line
of the sidebar in the Studio. docs/editing-content.md has the detail for each kind
of content.

Words used here:

- **Publish** saves a change so the TV can see it. A change you have not published
  is a draft, and the TV never shows a draft.
- **Studio** is the page where you edit. **Sidebar** is the list of lines on its left.
- **Mini** is the small computer behind the TV. It reads Monday and the calendars
  for the TV.
- **Panel** is one block on the TV, such as the agenda or the photos.
- **Coach** means a coach or mentor of the team.

## What the TV shows

The TV shows five areas, A to E. Start here draws them as a picture, with the
sidebar lines that fill each one.

| Area | What it shows | Where you change it |
|------|---------------|---------------------|
| A Banner | The team name and number, the school, the date, the time and the weather | Teams and Dashboard Settings, for coaches |
| B Main panel | The big panel. It takes turns showing the agenda, the events, the Monday task cards, the team leads, the leadership, the sponsors, the photos and the extra panels | Daily Agenda, Calendars, Monday Tasks, Team leads, Leadership, Sponsors, Photos, Extra panels |
| C Countdown | The time left to Kickoff, then to Rollout | Dashboard Settings, for coaches |
| D Side panel | The small panel under the countdown. It takes turns showing the next event, the task counts, the safety days, a sponsor logo and the forecast | Monday Tasks, Sponsors, Calendars, Dashboard Settings |
| E Ticker | One tip, news item or reminder at a time along the bottom | Tips and News, Sponsors |

Two teams use the TV, Prime and Nova. The TV swaps between them. An item with the
Team field left blank shows for both. An item for one team shows only while that
team is on the screen.

A coach chooses which Screen plays each day and which panels play in each area, in
Screens, the second line of the sidebar. You do not need to touch it.

Some things cover the whole TV. A booked talk takes the full screen at its time.
An alert does too. The night screen is dark from 11:30 pm to 11:30 am.

## Every meeting

1. Open the line you need in the sidebar.
2. Make the change.
3. Click **Publish all** in the top bar.

You edit on Draft, and the TV shows Published. Publish all publishes every draft
that is filled in properly. It names any draft it skipped and says what is wrong
with it. The TV changes within a few seconds.

To take something off the TV without losing it, turn off Show on screen and publish.
Hide after is an optional date that takes it down by itself.

If a change does not show, check four things. It is published. Show on screen is on.
Hide after has not passed. The Team choice is Both, or the team that is on the screen
(docs/team-on-an-item.md).

## Tasks

Tasks come from the team's Monday boards. Nobody types a task in the Studio, so there
is no plus button. The Mini reads the boards on a timer and makes a task for each item.
The reading takes up to 30 minutes, so a change in Monday is not on the TV at once.

Your task shows on the TV when it has a department, a team or General, Show on TV is Show, and it is not a subitem.

- **Department** is the Department column in Monday. A task whose department is
  Unassigned never shows. A new task gets a department from its group, and the
  automation can take up to 30 seconds.
- **Team** is the Team column: Prime, Nova or General. General shows for both teams.
- **Show on TV?** is a column. Show is the starting value. Hide takes the task off.
- **Subitems** never show. Put the work you want on the TV on a parent item.
- **Status** comes from the Status column, not from the group. A task you reopen
  stays in the Done group and still reads as To Do.
- **POC** is the person in charge. Only the first name shows. Pick one person.
- **Timeline** and **Estimated Hours** feed the due date, the overdue mark and the
  progress numbers. A task with no hours counts as one hour.

In the Studio, Monday Tasks lists the tasks in two lists, On the TV and Hidden.
Each task opens read only. The one thing you can change is **Show on TV**. Monday
Boards shows every board the Mini can see, with when it was last read. If a Monday
panel shows a STALE chip, the Mini has not read the boards for a while. Tell a coach.

## Events

Events are not typed in the Studio. They come from the team calendars. To get an
event on the TV, add it in the calendar app. It reaches the TV within 15 minutes.
The Calendars page, under Events, shows what each calendar brings in and whether the
TV shows each event. If an event is in the calendar and not on the TV, a Calendar
filter may be hiding it, or it may be later than the events that fit. Ask a coach for an Always show
rule (docs/calendar-filters.md).

## Photos

1. Open Photos and click the plus button.
2. Add the picture. A large photo from a phone is fine.
3. Use the crop tool. Drag the circle onto the main subject, such as a face.
4. Caption is optional. Credit is optional and is the first name of the person who
   took the photo.
5. Click Publish. A photo shows on the TV as soon as it is published, so look at it first.

Rules: first names only, with no last name in a caption. Switch location tagging off
on your phone before you take a photo. Take it in landscape. Upload only a photo that
may be shown, because anyone who has the address of a photo can open it
(docs/editing-content.md, "Photos").

## Booking a presentation

A talk is booked in the Google Form: [link to the Form, added by a coach].

1. Open the Form and pick a free slot. Slots close for booking 30 minutes before they start.
2. Type your first name, your subteam, a title for the talk and the link to your
   Google Slides deck. The title and the first name are public, so write what you
   are happy for anyone to read.
3. Share the deck with Anyone with the link, set to Viewer. The TV cannot show a deck
   that is not shared.
4. On the day, be at the TV at your start time. A title card comes up. Press the
   clicker (Page Down) to begin. Page Down goes forward and Page Up goes back. A left
   click works too, and a right click goes back. If nobody presses a key within 5 minutes, the
   talk is skipped.
5. After the last slide, one more press shows a thank you card, and the TV goes back
   to its panels.

To cancel or move a talk, ask a coach. They set the status to Cancelled or change
Starts at (docs/presentations.md).

## The six buttons

The buttons on the Start here page try the screen without waiting for a time or for a
booked talk. Each sends a request, and the TV answers within a few seconds.

- **Play announcement:** plays every full-screen announcement that is switched on, once.
- **Run presentation test:** runs a sample talk with six sample slides. Press the
  clicker as at a real talk.
- **Next look now:** moves the main panel on to its next row, which can change the team
  or the Screen.
- **Preview competition:** shows the competition cards for 2 minutes, on sample data.
- **Post an announcement:** opens a new announcement for the bar under the banner. It
  starts now and ends in two hours.
- **Preview the screen:** opens the screen with sample content in a new tab. The address
  is the Mini's own, so on another computer the tab shows no page.

If the page under the buttons says Dashboard Settings has not been published yet, ask a
coach to open it and click Publish once.
