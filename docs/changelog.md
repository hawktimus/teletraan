# Changelog

One line for each round, from `git log --oneline`. The log has no round names, so the rounds are cut by the dates of the briefs. A commit has the hash that `git log` shows. To see one round, use `git log --since` and `--until` with its dates.

- **Build and round 1 (4 to 10 October).** The first dashboard, then purple plates with metal edges, the page changes, the logo, night mode, the hidden transitions, Sanity photos and the connection check; seasonal packs and the Neon Prime theme; Meeting days, presentations with the slides fetcher and mouse control; calendar filters; teams and Nova; styles, the mirror, layouts, the war clock and Cybertron; the Studio sidebar in groups, the Start here page and Dashboard Settings in tabs; the boot and shutdown screens; the look rotation; events on two pages with kinds; the FRC feed and competition cards; the first Monday fetcher and Monday cards; and the students and coaches docs.
- **Round 2 (10 to 11 October).** The Mac tool for the Mini, team plates, body, neon and metal, one pull apart and re-form swap for every change, the Monday cards as rows, a photo that fills the panel, portrait size, shape and frame settings, the Monday block drawn in its own style, the event chip only when a kind is set, no plate ids, Panel B always drawn in the standard layout and a blank team that means both teams.
- **Round 3 (11 to 12 October).** The Screen documents with the map, the sections and the weekday schedule, the countdown title on its own line, the Leadership role as a chip, the Mac tool words update, status and restart, the Ship app, the laptop setup guide, the hawk that turns into the bird every five minutes, the Events panel that scrolls to its next four, and the title plate line.
- **Round 4.** The Monday design boards. The log has no commit of its own for it; its boards are the input of round 5.
- **Round 5 (15 October).** The new Monday reading with two boards and a read interval, the Monday rules and the stale chip, six Monday panel kinds, the panel preview in the Studio, the screenshot tool, the competition preview and the fixture tool, announcements, the booking check, the Photos row, the kiosk watchdog, the nightly dataset backup, the build on the wall, the sweep, the student guide and the task one-pager.
- **Round 5.5 (15 October).** The kiosk finds the display and sets a 1920 by 1080 framebuffer, and the TV no longer runs old code after an update. Nova became the inverse of Prime, gold and purple in gold metal.
- **Round 6, folders (15 October).** `deploy/` became `mini/`, the Ship app moved to `tools/mac`, each panel went to `dashboard/panels/<id>/`, the Studio inputs and tools got their folders, and every test moved to `tests/` with one runner (docs/paths.md).
- **Round 6, the control page and the views (15 to 16 October).** The nightly restart with the skip for booked talks; the control service with the PIN, releases, roll back, the zip upload, the install steps, the Studio build on the Mini, the helper socket, `mini/install.sh` and the git path; the Now showing, Status, Keys and Log tabs and the three dots on the TV; four views (General, Prime, Nova and Family) in place of the Screens runtime, with the Views and Themes pages, the weekday themes, Test the token and a Start here that shows status and links; themes in folders with Retro as a marked copy of Original, and Minimal no longer a theme; the inverted palette for Nova; subthemes; and the weather that blanks after two hours.
- **Round 6, review.** Every script in `mini/scripts` starts the same way and reads `lib.sh`, the long scripts are split into parts, the control service has one reader of `local.env`, the docs follow the folders, the views and the themes, and this changelog was written.
- **Round 6, names.** The computer in the classroom is the Teletraan computer, not the Mini: `mini/` became `computer/`, `tools/mini/` became `tools/teletraan/`, `teletraan-mini` became `teletraan-tool`, and three docs and four tests changed name with them (the list is in docs/paths.md, "Renames").
- **Round 6, the Studio sidebar.** The sidebar goes by what people do (SCREEN, TASKS, EVENTS, PEOPLE, COACHES ONLY). Rotation and Themes and backgrounds are pages of their own, the old Screens and Family are in Old screens (not used), the tab Look of Dashboard Settings is Logo and motion, the pages fit a phone, and a Team has the action Use the standard colors for this team. docs/studio-map.md has the sidebar.
- **Studio redesign, Photos.** The Photos page picks where the Photo panel gets its pictures (Upload photos, Google Drive folder or Both), adds many photos at once and shows how the last read of a Drive folder went. The Teletraan computer copies the pictures of a shared Drive folder every 15 minutes with a Google API key (docs/photos-drive.md).
- **FRC news.** The Teletraan computer reads the FIRST community blog feed every 2 hours and keeps the newest headlines of the chosen categories (FRC and Control System to start), headline, date and categories only. The FRC news panel shows five of them with a SystemCore chip, a STALE chip when the list is old, and the ticker can carry the newest three. The Competition page has an FRC news section with a Show switch for each headline, and the control page has Sync news now (docs/frc-news.md).
- **Round 7, the Studio by what is on the screen (17 October).** The sidebar has one line for each thing the audience sees: Today (the alert, the bar announcements and the full screen messages), Ticker, Events, People, Photos, Monday, Teams and views (the two teams on a plain form, with the standard colors button), Look (one page for the theme of each weekday, the background, the decorations, motion, polish and the logo), Competition, and under Coaches only Dashboard Settings, Extra panels and Kept data. Dashboard Settings has five tabs, and the fields the new pages took over are hidden with their stored values. The old Screens and the Condensed view are on Kept data.
- **Round 9, Before and after.** The Competition cards setting has a fourth mode, Before and after (stored as `around`, the start for a settings page with no stored mode): a preview from 14 days before an event, nothing while it is on and a recap for 7 days after. Three new cards, Event preview (with the strength of the field by EPA and the season trend), Our matches (chances, expected record, hardest and easiest match) and Event recap, and the frc-sync fields they read (`schedule` and `field`, Statbotics ratings kept 12 hours with a limit of 60 a run).
- **Match13 forecasts.** A new key, MATCH13_API_KEY, in the Keys section of the control page and in the Mac tool. `match13-sync.sh` (timer every 30 minutes, decides by itself) reads Match13 for our teams and writes `match13.json` in the data folder of the Teletraan computer, never to Sanity. Two new competition cards, Forecast and Chances, and the helpers `matchForecastFor` and `fieldStrengthFor` for the match cards. Sync Match13 now and Last Match13 read on the control page, a Match13 block on the Competition page in Studio. See docs/match13.md.
- **Display tab on the control page.** The control page has a third tab, Display: the rotation rows as cards that move up and down, with the view, panel, seconds and Show on screen of each row, the theme of each weekday, and for each row a plain line saying whether the wall would show it now or skip it and why, with the Monday tasks, FRC news posts, events and TBA lines it would draw. It reads Dashboard Settings from Sanity, saves only the fields it changed to the draft with the same stored names as the Studio, asks before it overwrites a newer Studio edit, and publishes with its own button. The write key stays on the Teletraan computer. Match13 matches are read from the local file and shown only on this page. See docs/control-page.md.
- **Multi-level Monday boards.** The Monday sync lists boards with `hierarchy_type`, marks a multi-level board in the list, and reads one with a question that also asks for `BatteryValue` (the key of a label and a count) and the label settings of the columns, so department, status, team, priority and Show on TV work as on a classic board. Classic boards (Build Tasks, Milestones) are read as before. The API version is one named line, now 2026-01, and has not been tried with the live token. `monday-sync.sh --debug <board>` and the helper word `monday-debug` print the kind of board, the version and the shape of the first two items with no token and no names of people, as the Monday debug button in Settings, Monday on the control page. The Studio buttons Test the token and Check the token, which called an address that does not exist, are now a note and a link to the control page, and `controlAddress` has the port 3229. See docs/monday.md, Multi-level boards.
- **Quick hide on the Display tab.** A group above the rows with Hide all Nova rows and one hide or show button for each panel in the list (Robot Status and 3D view first), each saying how many rows it changes. They only switch Show on screen in the page's copy, and Save to draft and Publish to the wall send it. See docs/control-page.md.
- **Monday group as department.** The Monday sync asks for the group of each item (`group { id title }`, in every item question of `monday-contract.sh`). When the Department column is empty or Unassigned and the group title matches a department label of the settings, ignoring capitals, the task takes that department. A task with the column set keeps it, and a group like Done changes nothing. No new field is stored. See docs/monday.md, The Show on TV rule.
- **Monday, rebuilt simple.** The Teletraan computer reads two boards by their numbers, Robotics Tasks and Completed Tasks (`computer/scripts/monday-contract.sh`), parents only, in pages, and writes one task for each with its department (the group, else the Department column), its list (Done for a Done status, the Done group or Completed Tasks, else Open), status, team, the first name of the first person in Assigned, priority, the end of the Timeline, Date Completed and the hours. A task whose item is gone is deleted. The milestones board and the list of every board are not read any more. Two panels replace the eleven Monday panels: Department tasks (Status, Project, Contact, Priority, Estimated Completion Date, for one department, Open or Done, both teams or one) and the TV charts (status by department, overall progress by hours, timeline). They are chosen on the Rotation board and the Display tab with dropdowns. The older panels are retired: a stored row plays nothing and says so, and an older tasks row with a department plays as Department tasks. The standard order has the nine departments and the three charts. The Monday page of the Studio is one read only page, and the board pickers, the column settings and the token buttons are hidden. See docs/monday.md.
- **Technical deep dive.** `docs/technical-deep-dive.md` describes the whole system as of commit 1b44659 for mentors, sponsors' engineers and other teams (purpose, data flow, the Teletraan computer, the dashboard, the Studio, the control page, Monday, competition, tests, deployment, security, risks, where the docs and the code disagree, how to extend, a glossary), and `docs/technical-deep-dive-short.md` is the 900 word summary.
- **Monday page by team and department.** The Monday page of the Studio has a Team picker (Both, Prime or Nova) and, for each of the nine departments, the To Do and In Progress tasks and the Done tasks in the five columns of the TV with counts, empty departments greyed, a line about the last read, and What the TV shows, the first page of a chosen Department, List and Team drawn with the dashboard's own `departmentView` (imported, not copied). A phone shows rows as cards. The Kept data links are 44 pixels high. See docs/monday.md.
- **Team metrics and Compare.** Two competition cards that join frc-status and the Match13 file for Team 3229 (Prime) and Team 3426 (Nova): Team metrics for the team on the screen (season record, rank, ranking points, average score, win streak, Match13 rating and its percentile, auto, teleop and endgame points, OPR and DPR, the next match with its win chance and the average rating of partners and opponents, the matches still to play, the last three results and the events of the season) and Compare with the two teams side by side in their own colors. They come in Before and after and in Always, show NOT YET for a number that is not there, are in the Rotation board as an automatic card (Team metrics also as a row, with a team) and in the Display tab, and have a switch each on the Competition page. No new data is read. See docs/frc-feed.md and docs/match13.md.
- **Guard rails and the first change.** `docs/your-first-change.md` walks a student through a color, a ticker line, a panel's seconds, a field, a new small panel, the tests and the Ship app, each step tried on a Mac. `tests/house-rules.test.mjs` fails with the file, the line and what to do instead when a panel holds animation code, the dashboard holds a framework or a build file, text in a panel is under 44px, there is a shadow, a blend mode, 3D or video, a panel folder disagrees with `registry.js`, or a file that configures a code writing tool is kept. `tests/stored-names.test.mjs` fails when a stored field, type or choice is renamed or removed (`--add` records new names), and `tests/no-secrets.test.mjs` fails when a key, a token, a private address or a value of `local.env` is in the repository. `node tests/run.mjs --quick` runs the fast tests in about half a minute. A failing test now shows its message first, the rotation default test names the row that differs, and the panel loader and the clock say in the console what to check. `docs/adding-a-panel.md`, `docs/adding-a-field.md` and `docs/where-things-are.md` match the real files again.
- **Content on the Display tab.** A Content section under the Display tab with three blocks that fold. Ticker and news edits the Tips and News lines (add, edit with the character counter, Hide and Show, order, delete) with a preview of how the ticker draws each line, and lists the FRC news headlines with a Hide button each. Calendar lists the coming BAND events by day, says which Calendar filter hides each one, and hides or shows an event with a draft one event rule like the Studio Calendars page, with a Publish button. Roster lists a new Studio type, Roster person (first name, role, job title, team, department, portrait, Show on screen, Hide after), by team and role with counts, and lists the Leadership and Team leads read only. The control service got collection routes (list, new draft, save, publish and delete after a confirm) for the types the plan names as collections. Old truths changed: the sidebar title Roster is no longer one of the old titles (it is the new list under People), and Tips and News left the list of types the Control page does not edit. See docs/control-page.md and docs/roster.md.
- **Check the screen.** A coaches only page that reads the published documents and the drafts and lists, by area, what would make the TV look bad or show nothing: text too long for its panel, a web address, email or phone number in public text, a full name where a first name belongs, photos that are small or taller than the panel, photos with no first name credit, leaders with no role or photo, sponsors with no logo, agenda items with no date, announcements that end before they start, items past their Hide after date, repeated lines, calendar filters that match every event, team colors that are hard to read, and Rotation rows that the wall will skip or that show one topic twice. Each finding has a level, a plain fix and a link to the document. The same rules are yellow warnings in the schemas while typing. The limits are in `studio/schemas/limits.js` and the checks are plain functions in `studio/tools/content-check.js`, tested in `tests/content-check.test.mjs`. See docs/editing-content.md.
- **Scheduled reboot.** The Teletraan computer can reboot at a time of day that the owner sets. Five new fields of Dashboard Settings (`rebootEnabled`, off to start with; `rebootTime`, 03:30; `rebootDays`, every day; `rebootSkipBusy`; `rebootWaitMinutes`, 30) are in a Scheduled reboot section of the Advanced tab in the Studio, with the next and last reboot at the top, and in a Scheduled reboot section of Settings on the control page, which saves to the draft, asks before it publishes and shows the same values. `teletraan-reboot.timer` wakes `scheduled-reboot.sh` every 5 minutes; `reboot-plan.mjs` decides in the time zone of the Look page, waits while a talk, an alert or an announcement is playing and reboots anyway after the wait, keeps the last setting for an outage, reboots a slot once, never within 10 minutes of boot and never within 6 hours of the last reboot, and writes the next and last reboot to `status-mini`. `install-reboot.sh` is run by the Mac tool and needs `curl`, `jq` and Node 18 or newer. The Sunday reboot of `nightly.sh` is unchanged. See docs/control-page.md.
- **Performance.** Measured on the sample content with the CPU slowed 4 and 6 times: frame times at each panel change, memory over 600 row changes and a 21 minute run, start up, requests a minute, a trace and a profile (`tools/perf/measure.mjs`). Nothing leaks. `dateIn` made a new date formatter on every call and now keeps one for each time zone, which cut the script time of a quiet screen by more than half. A motion rule checks that row changes do not grow the page. docs/performance.md has the numbers, the budget and the findings for the animation code.
- **Pictures of a row on the Display tab.** A large live picture of the selected row beside the rows (above them on a phone) with its chip, its reason and the line What the TV shows now, and a small live picture on each row (at most 6 at a time, made only when the row is on screen, dropped while the tab is hidden, a phone has one for the selected row only), with a Show thumbnails switch that is remembered. A picture is the real dashboard in a sandboxed frame at 1920 by 1080, shrunk with a transform, on a new address `?rowpreview=<row as JSON>` that holds one row, still, and is read only (`dashboard/core/row-preview.js`, `row-preview-guard.js`): no rotation, takeovers, hidden effects, night screen, heartbeat or report, no write, no call to `/control/`, nothing kept in the browser. A held panel is now drawn again in place when the content changes, so the picture follows Sanity and Monday within seconds. The cards of the rows are patched, not replaced, so a change to one row does not load every small picture again. Opened on the Teletraan computer itself the page says so and draws nothing until asked. Measured cost for the person's browser, about 35 MB for each live picture and 3 percent of one core while still (docs/control-page.md). `tests/row-preview.test.mjs`, `tests/control-display-preview.test.mjs` and `tests/control-display-pictures.test.mjs` (a real browser, skipped without Playwright), `tests/helpers/display-world.mjs` and `tools/measure-display-pictures.mjs`.
- **Department tasks readable in both layouts.** The Robotics view already played Department tasks in the bar layout, but every preview of it (the Studio, the screenshot tool, the control page copy) drew the standard layout, where the Project and Contact columns were cut to three letters and the date name took three lines. A panel of the wide list is now drawn in the bar layout by a preview unless the address says layout=standard. The bar layout gives a first name 250 at 40px (it wraps, never cut), the last column is DUE DATE (the board still calls it Estimated Completion Date, and the Studio and the control page keep that name) and the project has room for two full lines. The standard drawing, which a view set to the standard layout, the Nova mirror and layout=standard still use, puts the contact under the status chip, has rows of 96, says DUE and gives the project 470. Old truths changed: the column names on the TV, the 40px size of the contact in the text size lists, and the address of a wide panel preview. See docs/layouts.md.
- **Championship chances off to start with.** The Chances card (Championship chances, Match13) has its switch off in the starting values (competitionQualify is false in defaultSettings, in the sample content and as the Studio starting value), so a new Dashboard Settings does not show it. The panel, its stored name and Preview competition are unchanged. A Dashboard Settings that already stores the switch on keeps showing it until a coach switches it off once on the Competition page. See docs/match13.md.
- **White flash hunt.** The page writes down each load with its reason (`dashboard/core/page-loads.js`) and sends the count of the last 24 hours and the last reason with its report; the Log tab of the control page shows them and the control log has a line for each load. `index.html` has a dark canvas and a black `html, body` background before any stylesheet, and no longer takes the style off while it swaps the build number into the stylesheet addresses. The Live preview takes no screenshot while the screencast works (it took a PNG at the start and every 30 seconds, and 4 screenshots a second in the fallback, which is now 1 a second). `kiosk.sh` sends the browser's errors to the journal and `KIOSK_GRAPHICS=plain` or `software` in `local.env` turns off the forced graphics flags. See docs/reliability-drill.md, "A white flash on the TV".
- **Jobs off root, less in the public dataset.** The watchdog and the nightly restart run as the login user and restart the screen or reboot through one sudo rule each. `computer/sudoers/teletraan-control` names every unit and command with no wildcard, and `tests/sudoers.test.mjs` and `tests/units-user.test.mjs` keep it so (only the shutdown screen runs as root, on a list with its reason). `calendar-status` lists a hidden event without its title, and a Monday task that says Hide is not written, or is deleted on the next read, with a count in `monday-status`. Run Install everything once (docs/updating-the-computer.md).
- **Transitions: one vocabulary for each theme, Light motion and fewer freezes.** The reference pages are explained in docs/transition-reference-guide.md. Foundry no longer plays the Original drawings and turn: it draws its own bolts, hasps, straps, rivets, cogs, pistons, teeth and steel plates, the Original look of the engine moved to `styles/original/reform-kit.css`, and a theme with no kit plays the plain kit (`core/reform-plain.js`). The Motion setting has a third value, Light (stored in the same field `motion`, with the older values unchanged): one simple change at each panel and view change, two plates and a few parts of the theme, no copies and no squares, and no hidden transitions. A second field, Motion on the TV computer (`motionOnKiosk`, light to start with), makes the Teletraan computer play Light when Motion says Full; the page knows it is that computer from `data/live/device.json`. `?motion=full`, `light`, `calm` or `none` on the address wins. A Full screen that misses its frame budget twice in a row plays Light from then on. The glint sweeps, the subtheme sprites, the seasonal pieces, the backgrounds and the gears rest while a transition plays and stand still in Light, the Foundry pistons no longer lay the page out on every frame, the screen speed check samples 40 frames every 10 seconds instead of asking for every frame, and a view transition draws about 70 squares instead of 130. Studio, Look, Motion and the Motion card of the Display tab set it. See docs/transitions.md and docs/performance.md.
- **Remove retired Monday rows.** The Display tab (Quick hide) and the Studio Rotation board have Remove retired Monday rows (N), which deletes the rows of the eleven older Monday panels from the list and nothing else, Add the nine Department tasks rows (Robotics, Open) and Add the three TV charts rows, which add a row for each department or chart that has none.
