# Using the Studio

For the students and mentors who change what is on the TV. No programming is
needed. This page has the same parts as the Start here page, the first line of
the sidebar in the Studio, so keep the two side by side. docs/editing-content.md
has the detail for each kind of content.

## What Teletraan I is

Teletraan I is the screen on the wall. The Studio is where we type what it
shows. Publish all is the last step of every change.

## The screen

The Start here page draws the screen as five boxes, A to E. Each box lists the
sidebar items that fill it. Some looks of the screen leave a box out or move it,
and a coach sets the looks. The boxes and what feeds them stay the same.

| Box | Area | What it shows | Where you change it |
|-----|------|---------------|---------------------|
| A | Banner | The team name and number, the school, the date, the time and the weather | Teams and Dashboard Settings, which are for coaches |
| B | Main panel | The big panel. It takes turns showing the tasks, the agenda, the events, the team leads, the leadership, the sponsors, the photos and the extra panels | Tasks, Daily Agenda, Calendars, Team leads, Leadership, Sponsors, Photos, Extra panels |
| C | Countdown | The time left to Kickoff, then to Rollout | Dashboard Settings, which is for coaches |
| D | Side panel | The small panel under the countdown. It takes turns showing the next event, the task counts, the safety days and a sponsor logo | Tasks, Sponsors, Calendars, Dashboard Settings |
| E | Ticker | One tip, news item, reminder or sponsor thanks at a time along the bottom | Tips and News, Sponsors |

## Every meeting

1. Open Daily Agenda or Tasks.
2. Make the change.
3. Click Publish all in the top bar.

You edit on Draft, and the screen shows Published. Publish all publishes every
draft that is filled in properly, and it names any draft it skipped and says what
is wrong with it. The TV changes within a few seconds.

- Daily Agenda, Agenda items: the schedule for today's meeting. Make one for
  each meeting, with its date. The panel is headed AGENDA (docs/up-next.md).
- Tasks: the plus button says Pin a task. A pinned task shows before the tasks
  that come from the team board in the same status. A task from the board is
  made by the Mini and opens read only. The one thing you can change on it is
  Show on TV (docs/editing-content.md, "Tasks from the team board").
- To take something off the screen without losing it, turn off Show on screen
  and publish. An optional Hide after date takes it down by itself.
- If a change does not show, check that it is published, that Show on screen is
  on, that Hide after has not passed, and that the Team choice is Both or the
  team on the screen (docs/team-on-an-item.md).

## Events

Events are not typed into the Studio. They come from the team's calendars. The
Calendars page, under Events, shows what each calendar brings in and whether the
TV shows each event, and has buttons to hide one.

To get an event on the screen, add it in the calendar app. It reaches the TV
within 15 minutes. If it is in the calendar app and still not on the screen, a
Calendar filter may be hiding it, or it may be later than the next eight events.
Ask a coach for an Always show rule (docs/calendar-filters.md).

## The five buttons

The buttons on the Start here page try the screen without waiting for a time or
for a booked talk. Each sends a request and the screen answers within a few
seconds.

- Play announcement: plays every announcement that is switched on, once.
- Run presentation test: runs a sample talk with six sample slides. Press the
  clicker as at a real talk (docs/presentations.md).
- Next look now: moves the screen on to the next look.
- Preview competition: shows the competition cards for 2 minutes, on sample data.
- Preview the screen: opens the screen with sample content in a new tab. The
  address is the Mini's own, so on another computer the tab shows no page.

If the page under the buttons says Dashboard Settings has not been published
yet, ask a coach to open it and click Publish once.

## Coaches only

Anything under Coaches only (Settings and Extra panels) is for a coach, so ask a
coach. The coaches' page is docs/coaches-guide.md.
