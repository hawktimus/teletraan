The hawk logo and the team name

The logo is drawn by dashboard/core/logo.js and moved by the section "The logo" of dashboard/frame.css. Its acts are played by dashboard/frame.js (the Logo tab of Dashboard Settings sets when). The team name is built by dashboard/core/name.js and split by the section "The team name" of frame.css. Press a button to play one act, or drag the slider to stop an act at any moment. It is all explained in docs/the-logo.md.

The whole show is the real thing, with the starting values of Dashboard Settings: the entrance, then the flying hawk every 24 seconds (the first at 13), the turn every 72 (the first at 50) and the name effect when it is due. In calm and none motion the logo stays the still emblem.

The Studio allows 16 characters. Try a long name of wide letters: the letters squeeze together so the name never runs into the clock.
